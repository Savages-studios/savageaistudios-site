What the pilot checks

Structured input

Cases must fit FacturForge's current JSON invoice input contract. This is not OCR, PDF extraction, or automatic import from an order export.

Bounded generation and validation

FacturForge deterministically generates UN/CEFACT CII XML and reports findings for its documented rule subset. Unsupported profiles or cases are reported as out of scope.

Evidence packet

Receive case-level outcomes and a manifest tying delivered artifacts to the tested inputs and engine version, plus practical next steps for your own platform integration.

The boundary is explicit

FacturForge is a generation and preflight component.

It is not a French administration-approved platform, a PA/PPF connector, a Peppol access point, or a filing service. The pilot does not submit invoices, run the complete CEN Schematron rules or national CIUS, provide legal or tax advice, certify compliance, or guarantee acceptance by a recipient, platform, or authority.

French businesses must use an administration-approved platform for the applicable transmission duties. This pilot tests supported sample generation and findings; it does not replace that platform. Read the official approved-platform guidance.
